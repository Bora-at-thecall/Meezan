The provided text outlines Meezan, a digital platform designed for automated wealth management within the cryptocurrency sector. This system specifically caters to busy professionals and cautious investors who seek long-term market exposure without the stress of active trading. By utilising a non-custodial framework, the service ensures that users maintain complete control over their assets rather than relying on third-party custody. The product prioritises emotional discipline and simplicity, offering a streamlined user experience that functions independently for extended periods. Ultimately, it functions as a transparent portfolio tool rather than a speculative trading application, focusing on safe and stable asset allocation.
