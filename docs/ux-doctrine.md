Meezan – UX Doctrine
UX North Star:
Meezan should always feel like I am safe — never like I can lose everything with one mistake.
UX Law 1: Calm beats clever
No jargon. No charts by default. No visual noise.
UX Law 2: Two decisions max
Default onboarding requires only:
1) Choose risk level
2) Choose amount
Everything else is optional and hidden.
UX Law 3: Visibility without stress
Users see long-term progress, not daily volatility.
No red/green flashing. No candles.
UX Law 4: Neutral, reassuring language
No hype. No fear. No pressure.
Tone is quiet, professional, and respectful.
UX Law 5: Crypto is invisible
Gas, DEXs, tokens, contracts are hidden unless user expands “Details”.
UX Law 6: Mistakes must feel impossible
Clear actions.
Clear confirmations.
Easy withdrawals.
No scary warnings or dark patterns.
Design Reference:
Apple Health + Private Banking
Minimal. Slow. Confident.

This system specifically caters to busy professionals and cautious investors who seek long-term market exposure without the stress of active trading.
