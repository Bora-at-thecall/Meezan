Meezan – Product Definition (v1)
Product Name:
Meezan
One-line Description:
Meezan is a non-custodial, automated portfolio system that gives disciplined, long-term crypto exposure to people who no longer want to think, stress, or trade.
Primary User:
A busy Muslim professional, small family office, or individual traumatised by past crypto losses who wants long-term exposure with full control and minimal interaction.
What Problem Are We Solving:
Users want crypto exposure but:
- do not trust themselves to trade
- do not want to watch charts
- do not want emotional decision-making
- do not want custodial risk
- want something that keeps working even if they disappear for a year
Core Promise:
“Set it once. Stay in control. Let the system do the discipline.”
What Meezan IS:
- A calm, automated portfolio system
- Non-custodial and transparent
- Designed for years, not weeks
- Apple-grade UX: obvious, safe, minimal
What Meezan is NOT:
- A trading app
- A yield farm
- A strategy marketplace
- A prediction or signals tool
- A hype-driven crypto product
Portfolio Model (v1):
- One portfolio per user
- Risk-based presets by default
- Optional advanced custom allocation
